# Music and voice: what to generate and where to put it

## Music (MP3 or OGG, 60-90 seconds, seamless loop, no vocals, about 2-4 MB each)
Put offline music in `assets/music/`. Online world music goes in the online-content folder (see online-content/README.md).

| File | Prompt idea |
|------|-------------|
| assets/music/home.mp3 | cheerful bouncy mobile game menu music, ukulele, marimba and soft claps, playful, seamless loop, no vocals |
| assets/music/game.mp3 | calm focused puzzle music, light piano and soft synth pads, gentle pulse, relaxing, seamless loop, no vocals |
| online-content/ice-cave/music.mp3 | calm frozen cave ambience, music box and celesta, airy pads, soft wind, magical, seamless loop, no vocals |
| (later) jungle | warm jungle ambience, hand drums, wooden flutes, birds, playful, seamless loop, no vocals |
| (later) cave | mysterious underground ambience, deep pads, soft water drips, glowing crystal tones, seamless loop, no vocals |
| (later) space | dreamy space ambience, slow arpeggios, wide synth pads, twinkling sounds, seamless loop, no vocals |

Tip: ask the generator for "loopable" and check that the end matches the start. Music starts after the first tap on the screen (phones block autoplay).

## Voice clips (MP3, 0.5-1.5 seconds, `assets/voice/`)
Until you add them, the phone's own voice says the word. One energetic voice for all clips, ask for "excited game announcer, clear, short, dry (no echo)".
